## Key Findings

1. **Format-Specific Insights**
   - AVI, JPG formats show highest validity rates (>99%)
   - BMP format shows lower validity (67-70%)
   - PNG format shows moderate validity (~78%)

2. **Optimization Impact**
   - Constraints in -orig templates may reduce generation speed slightly
   - However, they improve file validity significantly in most cases

3. **Performance Tiers**
   - High-speed generators: MP4, PNG (~2500-2600 f/s)
   - Medium-speed: GIF, BMP, MIDI (~2000-2200 f/s)
   - Format complexity correlates with generation time

---

## Recommendations

1. Use optimized (-orig) templates for:
   - Targeted fuzzing with higher quality constraints
   - Compliance testing requiring mostly valid files

2. Use original templates for:
   - Broader fuzzing with less constrained generation
   - Edge case discovery

3. Format-specific notes:
   - AVI/JPG: Constraints are tight and effective
   - BMP: May benefit from additional constraint relaxation
   - MP4/PNG: Good balance of speed and validity
